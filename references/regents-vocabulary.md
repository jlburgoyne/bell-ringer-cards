# Regents vocabulary

The skill's controlled list of NYS ELA Regents terms, drawn from the New York State Education Department's Educator Guide to the Regents Examination in English Language Arts and the Part 2 and Part 3 rubrics. Definitions are paraphrased. Tag cards only with these IDs. Do not invent new terms.


## Exam Part

| ID | Term | Exam Part | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Reading Comprehension | Part 1 | 24 four-choice questions on one literary text, one poem, and one nonfiction text. 24 of 56 weighted credits. |
| P2 | Argument Essay | Part 2 | Argue a claim using at least three of four sources. 24 of 56 weighted credits. |
| P3 | Text-Analysis Response | Part 3 | At least two paragraphs on how a writing strategy develops a central idea or theme. 8 of 56 weighted credits. |

## Rubric criterion

| ID | Term | Exam Part | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| CA | Content and Analysis | Parts 2, 3 | Part 2: the claim and the analysis of sources, including the counterclaim. Part 3: the central idea and the strategy analysis. |
| CE | Command of Evidence | Parts 2, 3 | Choosing relevant evidence and using it to support ideas. |
| COS | Coherence, Organization, and Style | Parts 2, 3 | Focus on the task, organization of ideas, precise language, and sentence structure. |
| CC | Control of Conventions | Parts 2, 3 | Grammar, usage, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling. |

## Task skill

| ID | Term | Exam Part | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1-EVID | Best supporting evidence | Part 1 | Choosing the part of the text that best supports a central idea or claim. |
| P2-CLAIM | Claim | Part 2 | Stating a precise position on the question. |
| P2-COUNTER | Counterclaim | Part 2 | Distinguishing the claim from alternate or opposing claims. |
| P2-SOURCES | Evidence from multiple sources | Part 2 | Supporting the claim with evidence from at least three of the four sources. |
| P2-CITE | Citing by source and line | Part 2 | Identifying each source by number and line or graphic (e.g., Source 2, lines 4-6). |
| FORMAL | Formal style | Parts 2, 3 | Formal tone with no casual language or abbreviations, except in direct quotations. |
| P3-CENTRAL | Central idea or theme | Part 3 | Identifying a central idea or theme in the text. |
| P3-STRATEGY | Writing strategy analysis | Part 3 | Analyzing how the author's use of one writing strategy develops the central idea or theme. |
| P3-LINK | Linking evidence to the central idea | Part 3 | Connecting specific, relevant evidence to the central idea or theme. |
