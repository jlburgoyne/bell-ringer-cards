# Teacher profile

Fill this in for the teacher who will use the skill. Everything here is local context: the rest of the skill stays the same from teacher to teacher. The values below are examples from the classroom the skill was designed for; replace them.

## Classes

- **Grades taught:** 9, 10, and 11 (high school ELA, New York State).
- **Regents year:** 10th and 11th graders take the NYS ELA Regents this year, so one set serves both of those grades. 9th grade gets its own sets.
- **Class period:** 39 minutes, so a card takes about 3 to 5 minutes.

## How the cards are used

- Printed on plain paper, cut apart with a paper cutter, and pasted onto 3x5 index cards, then handed out at the start of class.
- Because cutting and pasting is the slow part, a card the teacher has to fix or reprint costs more than any other mistake.

## House rules

- **Written-response framework:** RACES. **R**estate the question, **A**nswer it, **C**ite text evidence, **E**xplain how the evidence proves the answer, **S**um it up. Use these terms the way the teacher does, so cards match the classroom poster. (Replace with the framework the teacher uses, such as RACE or ACE, or delete if none.)
- **Pronoun agreement:** in formal writing, a singular antecedent takes "his or her" (*Every student must bring his or her laptop*), or make the sentence plural. Teach and key it that way. (Replace with the teacher's or department's rule.)
